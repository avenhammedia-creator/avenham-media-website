Privacy and cookies.

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

  • Messages you send us through the contact form: your name, business, email, what you need and your message.
  • Page visits, counted per day, only if you choose “Accept all” on the cookie banner.
  • How you found us (for example Instagram or a Google search), only if you choose “Accept all”.
  • Error reports: if a page breaks, a short technical note so we can fix it. It contains no personal details.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your message and plan your project.
  • To see which pages help people, so we can improve the site.
  • To keep the website working.

Who can see it

Only the Avenham Media team. We never sell your details or share them for marketing.

How long we keep it

Messages are kept while we talk about your project and for up to two years after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Visit counts are kept for 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or object to how we use them. Email us at our contact address and we’ll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
